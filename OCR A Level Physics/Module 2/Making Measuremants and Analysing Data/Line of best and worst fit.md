# Line of best fit
This is the standard trend line 
## How to draw
- Pass as close to the center of as many data points as possible. 
- Ideally, have an even distribution of points above and below the line. 
- It represents your most "trusted" result.

# Line of worst fit
The steepest or shallowest straight line you can draw that still passes through all of your error bars. It is used to find the maximum possible uncertainty in your gradient. Here is how to draw it

## How to draw
- Look at the error bar of your **first** point (at \(d=0\)) and your **last** point (at \(d=50\)).
- To make a "Worst Fit" line, connect the **top** of the first error bar to the **bottom** of the last error bar (or vice versa).
- Ensure this line still passes through the vertical error bars of all the points in between.
