Below is an arrangement to find the permittivity of free space made from a large parallel capacitor.
![[Pasted image 20251208170501.png]]
The capacitor is charged is charged to a p.d of $V$. The charge $Q$ stored by the capacitor is measured by tapping a flying lead to the plate of the capacitor into a coulomb meter. 

Plot a graph of $Q$ against $V$ as the capacitor charges to different $p.d$s .

Draw a straight line best fit and take the gradient of the line.

Since $C = \frac{Q}{V}$  the gradient will be the capacitance. As well as that $C = \frac{\varepsilon A}{d}$. Thus the gradient will give you:
$$
\text{ (gradient) } m =\frac{\varepsilon A}{d}
$$
You can calculate the permittivity of free space in air as by:
$$
m\left( \frac{d}{A} \right) = \varepsilon
$$
