In Millikan's experiment, tiny electrically charged oil droplets were observed through a microscope. By altering the electric field strength between the capacitor plates, he was able to hold individual droplets stationary. For a stationary droplet, the force of weight pulling the droplet down was in equilibrium with the force of the electric field exerted on it, thus:
![[Pasted image 20251208172400.png]]
$$
mg = EQ
$$
Millikan found that the charge $Q$ on the droplets was quantised and was only values of $\pm ne$, where $n$ is an integer and $e$ is the elementary constant we all know.  The droplets acquire charge through friction at the nozzle of the spray, by either losing or gaining electrons. 

Thus, Millikan was awarded a Nobel prize in physics in 1929 for his work on determining $e$ and the photoelectric effect. 

However Millikan need to determine the radius $r$ of each droplet to determine its mass. 

Millikan's method for determining $r$ involved equating the equation for drag force at terminal velocity and rewriting $mg$ using volume and density.

The equation for the drag force $F_{d}$ on an object is given by:
$$
F_{d} = 6\pi \eta r v
$$
where $\eta$ is the viscosity of air and $v$ is the velocity of the object and $r$ is the radius of the object. 

We know from $\rho = \frac{m}{V}$ thus $m = \rho V$. Thus the mass of the object can be determine as the volume of the droplet and the density of the oil droplet $\rho$.

This is under assumption that the oil droplets are roughly spherical shaped at their small size. 

Thus m can be written as:
$$
\frac{4\pi r^{3}}{3}\rho =m
$$
With notion that $\frac{4\pi r^{3}}{3}$ is the volume of a sphere of radius $r$.

Substituting $m$ back into the equation $mg=F_{d}$ gives:
$$
\frac{4\pi r^{3}}{3}\rho g = 6\pi \eta rv
$$
Thus by measuring the velocity of the oil droplets at terminal velocity, Millikan was able to determine $r$:
$$
r = \sqrt{ \frac{9\eta v}{2\rho g} }
$$
From this Millikan was able to find the mass of the oil droplets and give us:
$$
Q =\frac{mg}{E}
$$
Which finally lead to Millikan discovering $Q$ was only quantised values of the the elementary charge constant $e$.
