# Earth's Magnetic Field
Earth has magnetic field like that of a bar magnet. It is caused by electrical currents circulating in the molten iron core of the Earth.

# Magnetic fields
A magnetic field is a field surrounding a permanent magnet or current carrying conductor.

Magnetic field lines are used to map magnetic patterns around magnets and current carrying conductors. 

- The arrow on a magnetic field is the direction in which a free pole would move. The arrows are from north to south
- Equally spaced and parallel magnetic field lines represent a uniform magnetic field
- Closer lines imply a stronger field

# Electromagnetism
A magnetic field is produced by moving charged particles, for example electrons moving within a wire.

# Current carrying conductors

## Long straight-current carrying wire

For a current-carrying wire, the magnetic field patterns are concentric circles on the wire perpendicular to it. 

![[Pasted image 20260113234621.png]]
The direction of the magnetic field can be determined using the right-hand grip rule. The thumb points in the direction of the conventional current, and the direction of the magnetic field is given by the direction in which the fingers curl around the wire.
![[Pasted image 20260105085416.png]]

## Solenoid and flat coil
The magnetic field of a solenoid and flat coil resembles the field pattern of a bar magnet. The magnetic field is uniform within the core of the solenoid and have north and south poles.
