For this question you must realise that charge is shared in proportion to the capacitance of each capacitor. This is due to the voltage being constant and the total charge across the capacitors being the sum of the individual charges. 
$$
\begin{gather*}
Q_{total} = Q_{1} + Q_{2} + Q_{3} +\dots. \\ \\
C_{total} = C_{1} + C_{2} + C_{3} +\dots. \\ \\
V = \frac{Q_{total}}{C_{total}}   = \frac{Q_{1} + Q_{2} + Q_{3}}{C_{1} + C_{2} + C_{3}}
\end{gather*}
$$
As the capacitance is fixed due being based on physical properties like area of plates and type of dielectric, charge is the only value that can change to for each capacitor to ensure overall voltage is constant. 

Comparing the proportions of capacitance. The capacitance of the uncharged capacitor is 1000 times larger than the ball. 
$$
\frac{C_{2}}{C_{1}} =\frac{ 1.2 \times 10 ^{ -8}}{1.2 \times 10^{-11}} = 10^{-3}
$$
Therefore the charge of the ball must now be smaller by a factor of $10^{-3}$ of it's original charge $Q_{0}$:
$$
\therefore Q = \frac{Q_{0}}{1000}
$$
