Ultrasound is longitudinal sound waves of frequency greater than 20kHz. 

Ultrasound is beneficial as it is non-ionising and non-invasive and quicker compared to methods using radiation. 

The frequencies for ultrasound is in the range 1-15 MHz. 

Ultrasound is refracted and diffracted at the boundary of two substances. Ultrasound in the human body has a wavelength of less than 1mm. This allows it to be used to identify features as small as a few millimetres. 

Ultrasound transducer both generate and receive ultrasound. Electrical energy is converted in sound and vice versa. This done through the piezoelectric effect. 

# Piezoelectric Effect
Crystals such as quartz produce an e.m.f when compressed, stretched or twisted. External forces that compress the signal induce an e.m.f. Likewise stretching forces cause the e.m.f to occur in opposite polarity.
