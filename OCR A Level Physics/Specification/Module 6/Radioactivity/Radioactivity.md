Investigations carried out in the 20th century showed that radioactive substances emitted $\alpha$, $\beta$ and $\gamma$ radiation. These types of radiation are described as ionising radiations as they can ionise atoms by removing some of their electrons, leaving positive ions.

A cloud chamber can be used to detect the presence of these types of radiation. 

# Properties and characteristics of the radiations
## Alpha
- Each alpha particle is a helium nucleus 
- An alpha particle has mass 4.00151u and a charge $+2e$. This makes it very ionising
