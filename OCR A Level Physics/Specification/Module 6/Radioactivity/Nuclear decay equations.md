The nuclei of **radium** atoms emit alpha particles and in doing so, they transmute (change) into new nuclei of **radon** atoms. The unstable radon nuclei in turn decay into nuclei of another element. This process stops when stable nuclei are formed. The nucleus before the decay is known as the parent nucleus and the new nucleus after the decay is called the daughter nucleus.
