For this problem you need to realise that the along the chain, beta decay begins. 
Only alpha and beta decay release particles. 

You can calculate the number of alpha particles from the change in nucleons:
$$
238 - 206 = \frac{32}{4} = 8 \text{ alpha particles}
$$
If there are $8$ alpha particles, you expect, $16$ proton decays. However there are 10 protons. Thus some of the decays are beta plus decays. Remember that beta decays takes a proton. Thus 6 beta 
$$
10 = 16 - n_{b} \implies n_{b} = 6
$$
plus decay particles were also released during the decay chain. Therefore the total number of released particles was $14$.
