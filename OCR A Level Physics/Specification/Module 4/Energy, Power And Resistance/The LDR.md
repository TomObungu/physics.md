A light-dependant resistor are small electrical components that change their resistance depending on the light intensity. 

A typical LDR is made from a semiconducting material that changes its number density e.g. by delocalising electrons, depending on the light intensity of the incident light. In dark conditions the intensity yields a very high resistance - the number density is very low. In bright conditions, the number density increases dramatically which leads to to an increase in current which in turn decreases resistance.
