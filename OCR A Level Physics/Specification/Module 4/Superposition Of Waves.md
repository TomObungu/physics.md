When two or more progressive waves meet, they combine and then pass through each other.  This process is called interference. When two waves pass each other and the resultant amplitude is decreased, it is called constructive interference. The opposite is destructive interference

## Principle of superposition
This states that when two or more waves meet at  a point, the resultant displacement is equal to the sum of the individual displacments at that point.
