Radio waves - $10^{6} - 10^{-1}$
Micro waves - $10^{-1} - 10^{-3}$
Infrared - $10^{-3} - 7 \times 10^{-7}$

Visible light 
- Red light $7 \times 10^{-7}$
- Blue light $4 \times 10^{-7}$

Ultraviolet - $4 \times10^{-7} - 10^{-8}$

X-rays - $10^{-8} - 10^{-10}$ 

Gamma - $10^{-13}$


# Properties of EM waves
- Can travel through a vacuum
- Travel at the speed of light $c$ in a vacuum
- Transverse waves
- Consist of oscillating magnetic and electric fields at right angles to each other.
