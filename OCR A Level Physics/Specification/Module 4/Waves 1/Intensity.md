The intensity of a progressives waves is defined as the radiant power passing at right angles through a surface per unit area. 

Intensity is measured in watts per square metre. The equation relating intensity $I$, area $A$ and incident radiant power is:
$$
I = \frac{P}{A}
$$
## Inverse square law
The intensity from a point source of power P decreases with distance $r$ from the source as the power spreads over a larger surface area. The intensity is given by:
$$
I = \frac{P}{4\pi r^{2}} \propto \frac{1}{r^{2}}
$$
## Intensity and amplitude
The intensity $I$ for a wave is related to the amplitude of $A$ of the wave by the following expression
$$
I \propto A^{2}
$$
