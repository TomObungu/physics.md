## Reflection
Reflection occurs when a wave changes direction at a boundary between two different media, remaining in the original medium. 

![[Pasted image 20260501064018.png]]

- The incident and reflected rays are in the same plane. 
- The angle of incidence is equal to the angle of reflection. The angles are measured relative to the normal. 

## Refraction
Refraction occurs when a wave changes direction as it changes speed when it passes from one medium to another.

- The frequency of refracted waves remains constant
- The speed and therefore wavelength of the wave changes. 

## Diffraction
Diffraction is the spreading of a wave when it passes through a narrow graph or around an obstacle 

![[Pasted image 20260501064452.png]]

- The speed, wavelength and frequency of a wave do not change when diffraction occurs
- There is significant diffraction when the width of the gap is similar to the wavelength of the incident wave. 

## Polarisation
Polarised waves have oscillations in one plane only. 

Longitudinal waves like sound cannot be polarised as they are already limited to one plane of energy transfer
