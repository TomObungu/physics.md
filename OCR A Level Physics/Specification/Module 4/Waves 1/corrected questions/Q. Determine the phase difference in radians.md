The phase difference is always considered from the beginning of each wave cycle. In this case we can try $x=1$ as the beginning of the wave cycle. We can observe that the length of one wavelength, $\lambda$, is $2cm$ and the distance between the points at $x=1.5$ and $x=2.5$, $x$, is $1$. Thus from the phase difference equation:
$$
\begin{gather*}
\phi = \frac{x}{\lambda} 2\pi \\ \\
\phi = \frac{1}{2} 2\pi = \pi
\end{gather*}
$$
