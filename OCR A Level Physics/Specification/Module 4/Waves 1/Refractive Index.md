The angle of refraction of light depends on the refractive index of each material

The refractive index of a transparent material is defined as the spped of light in a vacuum divided by the speed of light in the material. 

$$
n = \frac{c}{v}
$$
The refractive index for air by default is $n=1$. 


# Refraction law
The angle of incidence angle $\theta_{1}$ and refracted angle $\theta_{2}$ are measured relative to the normal.
![[Pasted image 20260501084246.png]]
This is Snel's law:
$$
n_{1}\sin\theta_{1} = n_{2}\sin\theta_{2}
$$

 The refractive index of the glass block or secondary medium can be determined by the sine of the angle of incidence divided by the sine of the angle of refraction.
 $$
n = \frac{\sin i}{\sin r}
$$
