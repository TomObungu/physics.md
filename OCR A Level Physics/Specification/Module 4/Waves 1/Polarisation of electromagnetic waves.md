## Visible Light
Visible light can be polarised using polarising filters.  Polaroid sfilters are plastic fils that contain very long thin crystals that polarise light. Taking two vertical polarising filters and placing them in series with perpendicular orientations will cause no light to pass through. 

The intensity of light depends of the Maul's  law. Maul's states that the intensity of linearly polarised light is proportional to the square of the cosine of the angle between the light's polarisation direction and the polarizer's axis
$$
I = I_{0}\cos ^{2}\theta
$$

# Microwaves and Metal Grilles
Metal grilles act opposite to how standard optical polaroid filters works for visible light. 

Microwave transmitters produce plane polarised waves with the electric field oscillating in the vertical plane. 
![[Pasted image 20260501075434.png]]

- With a horizontal metal grille, the vertically plane polarised microwaves are transmitted through the grille. This is due to the electric field not interacting with metal and pass through the air gaps between the grille.
- With a vertical grille, the vertical electrical fields make direct contact with the metal grilles and and are absorbed by free electrons.
