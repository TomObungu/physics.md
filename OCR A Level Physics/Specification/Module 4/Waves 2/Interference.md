Coherence refers to waves emitted from two sources having a constant phase difference. 

All coherent sources emit waves of the same type, same frequency and wavelength. 

## Path difference and phase difference
The path difference is difference between the distances between two points on a wave:
$$
\text{path difference} = S_{1}P - S_{2}P
$$

Constructive interference occurs when the path difference is an integral multiple of $\lambda$. Constructive interference also occurs when the phase difference between two points is $0$ or a multiple of $2\pi$


Destructive interference occurs when the path difference is a non integral multiple of $\lambda$. It is also the case when the phase difference is $\pi$.

## Interference in sound 
Two loudspeakers to the same signal generator will emit coherent sound waves. The sound waves travel out form each loudspeaker and overlap, forming an interference pattern. 

The interference pattern comprises of a series of maxima and minima.

## Interference in microwaves
A coherent microwave source can be produced sending microwaves through a double. The diffracted microwaves overlap and form an interference pattern that can be detected with a microwave receive connected to a voltmeter or oscilloscope
