The area under a graph of gravitational field strength $g = -\frac{GM}{r^{2}}$ represents the change in  gravitational potential  $\Delta V_{g}$

![[Pasted image 20251107191302.png]]This because because for a uniform shape the $$
area \approx gr = -\frac{GM}{r^{2}}r \approx V_{g}
$$
$\Delta V_{g}$ can be useful for problems when needing to calculate velocities in [[Finding impact velocity using Gravitational Potential]] and for other problems.
