The Big Bang theory attempts to describe the origin and development of the early Universe. The theory suggests that are some point in the past all the matter in the Universe was contained in a single point - A singularity. This region was much hotter and denser than it is today. From that point the Universe has been rapidly expanding into what we see today.

# Evidence for the Big Bang
The two main pieces evidence for the Big Bang are
- Hubble's law 
- Cosmic background microwave (CBM) radiation

Hubble's law shows that the Universe is expanding as galaxies are receding from each other because the space itself is expanding in all dimensions. If we were to reverse this process, the Universe would return to a single denser and hotter point.
