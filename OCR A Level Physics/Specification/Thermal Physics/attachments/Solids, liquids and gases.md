The kinetic models describes molecular structure for the three states of matter - solids, liquids and gases.

In solid and liquid phases, the molecules experience, attractive electrostatic force and have  negative electrostatic potential. 

This means that external energy is required to pull apart, the molecules. 

The potential energy is lowest is solids, higher in liquids and highest in gases 0J.
