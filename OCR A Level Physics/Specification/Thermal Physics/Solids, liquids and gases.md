In solid and liquid phases, the molecules experience, attractive electrostatic force and have  negative electrostatic potential. 

This means that external energy is required to pull apart, the molecules. 

The potential energy is lowest is solids, higher in liquids and highest in gases 0J.

Electrostatic potential is described as the work done per unit charge in bringing a small positive test charge from infinity to that point. 

The equation follows the formula for voltage $\Delta W=q\Delta V$. This means the electrostatic potential.

This is because if there is a source of an electric field e.g electrons around the atoms $Q$. The work required by the electric field to push the external charge away from point A to B will equal to the Force of the electric field $F_{E}$ multiplied by the distance between $A$ and $B$. However, if we consider the work required required for the applied force to work against the force of the electric field $F_{E}$ we can see that this force will be negative.

Since the work done by the external force is against the charge of the electric field, we can define the electrostatic potential as this value of work done against the charge as $V=\frac{W}{q}$.

# Properties of different matter phases
# Solids
Solids vibrate around fixed positions, this means they have the lowest kinetic energy (KE). The molecules are very close to each other.
Due to the force of electrostatic force of attraction between the molecules being very strong, they have lower negative value of electrostatic potential energy (PE). (In this case since the smaller values are negative, then values closer to 0 and greater and thus have greater electrostatic potential)

## Liquids
The molecules are not fixed and can move around each other
This means that the liquids have a greater KE, but due to a weaker electrostatic force of attraction between the molecules. The particles have a greater electrostatic potential energy. This also implies the the liquids have a greater mean separation.

# Gases
Gas molecules can move freely and rapidly and are spread very far apart from each other and move very quickly. This means that they have the highest KE and experience negligible electrostatic force. This also means that they have the highest potential energy of 0J.
