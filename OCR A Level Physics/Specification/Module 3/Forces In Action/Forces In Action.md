A resultant force $F$ acting on a object of mass $m$ will produce acceleration $a.
$$
\begin{gather*}
\text{resultant force } = mass \times \text{acceleration}  \\ \\
F = ma
\end{gather*}
$$
**The Newton is defined as the resultant force that will give a mass of $1kg$ acceleration of $1ms ^{-2}$**
