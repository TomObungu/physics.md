## Torque
The torque of a couple is defined as the product of one of the forces and the perpendicular separation between the forces.
$$
\begin{gather*}
\text{torque}= Fd \\ \\
 torque = \text{one of the forces } \times \text{perpendicular separtation between the forces }

\end{gather*}

$$

The torque is the same as the total moment of the two forces about their midpoint, $c$.
