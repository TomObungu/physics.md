A body is in equilibrium when the forces acting on the object do not produce any rotation or any acceleration. No acceleration means that resultant force must be zero.

# Moment of a force
The product of the force applied and the perpendicular distance of the line action of the force from the pivot.

$$
m = Fx
$$

# Principle of moments
- Resultant force = 0
- Resultant moment = 0

The sum of the clockwise moments about a point or an axis equal to the sum of the anticlockwise moments about the same point or axis.

The figure below shows a uniform beam pivoted at its centre of gravity. It is in equilibrium and balanced horizontally by two weights $W_{1}$ and $W_{2}$. 

![[Pasted image 20260101004803.png]]

When taking moments about the pivot, we get:
$$
W_{1}d_{1} = W_{2}d_{2}
$$
