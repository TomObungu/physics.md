Objects moving through air will experience a resistive force drag and an upthrust. The resultant force on a the object is no longer its weight and therefore its acceleration is no longer $9.81$

# Drag
The term drag is used for resistive force experienced by an object moving through a fluid. The factors that affect drag are:
- The speed of an object - the greater the speed, the greater the drag
	- More importantly $F \propto v^{2}$
- The cross-sectional area at right angles to the direction of motion
- The viscosity of the fluid 

# Terminal velocity
The maximum velocity of an object that occurs when the
resistive and driving forces acting on the object are equal to each other.

## Just before falling
![[Pasted image 20260101004001.png]]
- Speed is 0
- Drag is 0
- Resultant force is weight $F=mg$
- Acceleration is $g$

## During the fall
![[Pasted image 20260101004041.png]]
- Speed > 0
- 0 < drag < mg 
- Resultant force = mg - drag
- Acceleration = $\frac{mg-\text{drag}}{m}$

## Terminal velocity
![[Pasted image 20260101004143.png]]
- Speed is constant
- Drag = weight
- Resultant force = 0
- Acceleration = 0
