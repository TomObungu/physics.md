For collisions in two dimensions:
- Total momentum in $x$-direction remains constant
- Total momentum in $y$-direction remains constant

Below is the collision between objects $A$ and $B$.

To solve problems like this, consider the momentum in the $x$ and $y$ directions independently

![[Pasted image 20260101084253.png]]- 

x-direction: initial momentum = final momentum:
$$
p = p_{1}\cos\theta + p_{2}\cos\theta
$$
y-direction: initial momentum = final momentum:
$$
\begin{gather*}
0 = p_{1}\sin\theta - p_{2}\sin\theta \\ \\
\therefore p_{1}\sin\theta = p_{2}\sin\theta
\end{gather*}
$$
