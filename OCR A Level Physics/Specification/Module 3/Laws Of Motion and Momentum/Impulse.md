Impulse is defined as the product of force and time.

According to Newton's second law, the change in momentum $\Delta p$ of a on object is given by:
$$
\Delta p = F \Delta t
$$
Therefore the change in momentum is the impulse. 

# Force-time graph
For a constant force acting for a time $t$, the impulse is equal to $Ft$. Thus the area under the force-time graph is equal to the impulse.
