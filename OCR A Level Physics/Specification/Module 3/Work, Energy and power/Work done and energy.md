A force does no work if there no motion.

# Work done
Work done is defined as the product of force $F$ and the distance $x$ moved in the direction of the force:
$$
W = Fd
$$
The unit for work is $Nm$ or $kgm^{2}s^{-2}$

1 Joule is defined as the the work done by a force of $1 N$ when the point of application of the force moves a distance of $1m$ in the direction of the force.
