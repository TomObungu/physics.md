Power is defined as the rate of work done
$$
P = \frac{W}{t}
$$
# Power and motion
Consider a car travelling at a constant speed $v$, on a level road. The constant speed means that the car has zero acceleration and the net force on the car must be equal to zero. 
![[Pasted image 20260101032308.png]]

The output power $P$ of the car can be determined as follows:
$$
\begin{gather*}
P = \frac{W}{t} \\ \\
P = \frac{Fd}{t} \\ \\
P = Fv
\end{gather*}
$$

# Efficiency 
The efficiency of a system or a device is defined as:
$$
\text{efficiency} = \frac{\text{usful output energy}}{\text{total input energy}} \times 100 \%
$$or:
$$
\text{efficiency} = \frac{\text{usful output power}}{\text{total input power}} \times 100 \%
$$
- Efficiency can be expressed either as a percentage or as a number between $0$ and $1$
