# Displacement
Displacement is defined as the distance in a given direction from a starting point.  

The magnitude of the displacement is the same as the distance travelled when an object travels in a straight line. 

# Velocity
Velocity is defined as the rate of change of displacement. The SI unit for velocity is the same as speed. 
$$
v  = \frac{\Delta s}{\Delta t}
$$

# Displacement-time graph
- The gradient of a s-t graph is equal to the velocity
