This question almost caught me out but I did get it in the end. Basically, you are given the distance of the ball and two times. Take:
$$
s = 5 \times 10^{-2} \qquad t = 4 \times 10^{-2}
$$
We can calculate the average velocity:
$$
\bar{v} = \frac{5\times10^{-2}}{4\times 10^{-2}} = 1.25 ms ^{-1}
$$
Now it is possible to calculate a value for $g$ through $a$ in $v=u+at$ as $v=1.25$m $u=0$ but we just need a value for $t$. Since we took the average speed, we take the average of the times and use that as the value for time:
$$
\bar{t} = \frac{0.10+0.14}{2} = 0.12
$$
Thus w can now $v=u+at$:
$$
a = \frac{v - u}{t} = 10.416666\dots \approx 10.41
$$
