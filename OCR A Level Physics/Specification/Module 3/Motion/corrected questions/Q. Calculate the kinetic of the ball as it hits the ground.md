This problem caught me out also because, I only considered the vertical component of the ball. In order for me to calculate the kinetic energy, I need to calculate the horizontal components and use Pythagoras to calculate $v$ and then use the kinetic energy equation:
$$
\begin{gather*}
u_{h} = 22.3\cos(6) = 22.2  \\ \\
\end{gather*}
$$
As the horizontal motion of a projectile is constant throughout the motion, the horizontal component of velocity is kept as $22.2$. From then we can calculate $v$
$$
v = \sqrt{ 22.2^{2}+7.25^{2} }  = 23.35
$$
Thus calculating $v$ gives:
$$
v = \frac{1}{2}(0.210)(23.35)^{2} = 57.2J
$$
