An object thrown vertically on the Earth will describe a straight-line path. An object thrown at an angle to the vertical will follow a curved path. 

# Independent horizontal and vertical motions
When air resistance is negligible, all projectiles on Earth will have an acceleration of $9.81$ vertically downwards.

- Horizontally: The horizontal velocity remains constant
- Vertically: The velocity changes and the motion can be described using equations of motion.
