You can measure the distance travelled by an object using a metre rule or a measuring tape. The speed of an object is related to distance time.

Speed is defined as the rate of change of distance. You can use the following word equation to determine the average speed $v$ of an object:
$$
\text{average speed} = \frac{\text{total distance travelled}}{\text{time taken}}
$$
or
$$
v = \frac{\Delta x}{\Delta t}
$$
# Distance-time graph
- The speed of an object can be determine from the gradient of the graph at time $t$
- For a curved distance-time graph,  the speed can be calculated determining the gradient of a tangent drawn to the graph

# Average speed
When given time and a distance travelled by an object $d$. The average speed of the object can determined by:
$$
\bar{s} = \frac{d}{t}
$$
It is then possible to estimate the final velocity for when the object is from rest, $u=0$:
$$
v = 2 \times \text{average speed}
$$
