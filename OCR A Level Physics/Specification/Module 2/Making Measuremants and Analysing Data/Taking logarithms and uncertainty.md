In physics, when you take the natural log of a number $y = \ln x$ the absolute uncertainty in the result $\Delta y$ is approximately equal to the **fractional uncertainty** of the original number.
$$
\Delta \ln N = \frac{\Delta N}{N}
$$

The value of $\ln N$ is:
$$
\ln 300 = 5.7037\dots
$$
The new absolute uncertainty is:
$$
\frac{\Delta N}{N} = \frac{40}{300} = 0.133333\dots
$$
