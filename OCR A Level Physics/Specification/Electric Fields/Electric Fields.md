A field a region in which an object will experience a force at a distance. An **electric** or **electrostatic** field is created by charged objects. Any other charged objects in this electric field will experience force. 

The strong electric field between the base of a thundercloud an the ground helps to channel the electric current of lightning. 

The build up and separation of positive and negative electrical charge between the base of thundercloud and ground causes an electric field.

## Lightning

As the difference in potential increases, the charge carriers gain enough energy from the electric field to be accelerated through the air and begin to ionise the air to a hot plasma until the current reaches the ground in a 'strike'. As well as that two consecutive charges may from the ground and from the base of cloud and meet in middle in a strike. This is The process happens almost instantaneously and produces very high currents.
