In this question you must first realise that there is a relative potential difference between the plates and that the electric field formed between the plates is uniform and thus the same at all distances:

1. Finding the relative p.d and the using $E=\frac{V}{d}$ to calculate $E$:
$$
\begin{gather*}
p.d = 4 \times 10 ^{ 2} - 1 \times 10 ^{ 2} = 3 \times 10 ^{ 2} \\ \\
E = \frac{3 \times 10 ^{ 2}}{15 \times 10 ^{-2}} = 2 \times 10 ^{ 3}NC ^{ -1}
\end{gather*}
$$

As the electric field is uniform, the electric field strength is both $2\times 10 ^{ 3}NC^{-1}$ at 5cm and 10cm. The direction is from the the lower to upper plate
