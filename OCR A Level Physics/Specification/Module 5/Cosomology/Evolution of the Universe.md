In 1999, physics was shaken by the discovery that the Universe appears to be expanding at an increasing rate. The most widely accepted theory included the concept of dark energy. This hypothetical form of energy fills all of space time and tends to accelerate the expansion of the Universe. This coupled with the discovery of dark matter, means that our best estimate of all that was see is only 5% of the Universe.

# Current idea about the Universe
- The Big Bang - Time and space are created. The Universe is a infinitely dense point - A singularity
- $10^{-35}$ s - The universe expands rapidly, including a phase of incredible acceleration known as inflation. All the matter in the universe is full of electromagnetic radiation in the form of high-energy gamma photons. The temperature of the universe is $10^{28}K$
- $10^{-6}$s  - Fundamental particles such as quarks and leptons gain mass through a mechanism involving the Higgs Boson.
- $10^{-3}$s - Quarks combine to form the first hadrons, such as protons and neutrons. Most of the mass within the Universe was created within the first second through the process of pair reduction. Pair reduction is high-energy photons transforming into particle-antiparticle pairs
- $1$s - The creation of matter stops after about 1s, once the temperature has dropped to about $10^{9}$
- $100$s - Protons and neutrons fuse together to form deuterium and helium nuclei, along with a small quantity of lithium and beryllium. The expansion of the Universe is so rapid that no heavier elements are created. During this stage, about 25% of the matter in the Universe is helium nuclei (known as primordial helium)
- $380 000$ years - The universe cools enough for the first atoms to form. The nuclei capture electrons. The electromagnetic radiation from this of the Universe is what can be detected as CBM
- $30$ million years - The first stars appear. Through nuclear fusion. In these stars the first heavy elements (beyond lithium) begin to form.
- $200$ million years - Our galaxy, the Milky Way forms, as gravitational forces pull clouds of hydrogen and existing stars together
- $9$ billion years - The solar system forms from the nebula left by the supernova of a large star. After the Sun forms, the remaining material forms  the Earth and other planets. It is thought that around $1$ billion years after the formation of the Earth. Primitive life forms on Earth
- Around $200000$ years ago the first modern humans evolve, and eventually study physics. The temperature of the universe is $2.7K$

# Dark Matter
In the late 1970s, astronomers were studying the Doppler shift in light from galaxies found that the velocity of stars did not behave as predicted. 
![[Pasted image 20251121020459.png]]
The observations can be explained if the mass of the galaxy is not in the centre. The current thinking is that there must be another type of matter which we cannot see. This dark matter is spread throughout the galaxy. Calculations have shown that the Universe must be made of 27% of this kind of matter.

Not much is known about dark matter. We do know that it cannot be seen directly with telescopes and that is neither emits nor absorbs light.
