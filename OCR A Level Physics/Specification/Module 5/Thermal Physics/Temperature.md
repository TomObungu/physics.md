## Triple point
The triple point of a substance is one specific temperature and pressure where the three phases of matter can exist in thermal equilibrium.  

This means that there is no net transfer of thermal energy between the phases. 

For water the triple point is $0.001^\circ$ and $0.61kPA$.

Temperature is a measure of hottness or thermal energy. An object increases in temperature when thermal energy is transfer into the object. This results in the object's molecular structure gaining kinetic energy and beginning to vibrate. This in turn also produces thermal energy. 

# Thermal equilibrium
When one object is hotter then there is a net flow thermal energy from the hotter object into the colder one.  

This increases the temperature of the colder object and lowers the hotter one. 

When two objects are in thermal equlibirum, there is not flow of thermal energy between them. 

This means any objects in thermal equilibrium must have the same temperature.

## Zeroth law of thermodynamics
The zeroth law states that if two objects are in equilibrium with a third, then all three are in thermal equilibrium. 

In other words, if A and B are in thermal equilibrium and B and C are in thermal equilibrium, then AC must also be in thermal equilibrium.

This forms the underlying basis of the definition of temperature - Objects have an underlying property, that determines the direction of any transfer of thermal energy. 

So this means, temperature determines the direction of thermal energy.

# SI Unit for temperature
The thermodynamic temperature scale for temperature is measured in $K$ (kelvin). 

Absolute zero has temperature of $0K$ and the triple point of water is $273.16K$

In other words, $273.15K$ is equivalent to $0C^\circ$:
$$
T(K) \approx \theta(C^\circ) + 273
$$
$$
\theta(C^\circ) \approx T(K) - 273
$$
