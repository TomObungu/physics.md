# Determining specific heat capacity
To calculate the specific heat capacity, you need to setup this circuit.

The energy transferred form the heater to the substance is given by $E=IVt$, where $I$ is the current in the heater, $V$ is the potential difference across the heater. And t is the time required, to increase the temperature. 

Once the circuit is set up, measure the current, voltage of the circuit. Before placing the oil inside the insulated container, measure the mass of oil inside a container. Once the mass is known, place it within the container and measure the initial temperature. Turn on the heater alongside the digital stopwatch. As the oil heats up, carefully stir the oil to ensure the temperature is uniform and measure the time in fixed intervals . This will reduce the uncertainty of temperature. After a set amount of time After a set amount of time e.g. 120 seconds. Turn off the heater and continue to measure the temperature until a maximum final temperature is found. Doing this will reduce uncertainty of the final temperature reading. With this information, calculate the Power, $P=VI$ and the energy transferred, $E=Pt$. The specific heat capacity can then be calculated using $c=\frac{E}{m\Delta\theta}$, where m is the mass of the block.
