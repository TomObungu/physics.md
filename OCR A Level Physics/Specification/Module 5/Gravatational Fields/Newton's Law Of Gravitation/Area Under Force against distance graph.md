For a graph of $F$ against $r$, the area under the graph is equal to the gravitational potential energy. 

This will be the case for two test masses resulting in a gravitational of attraction between as:
$$
F = -\frac{GMm}{r^{2}}
$$
![[Pasted image 20251211030733.png]]

The the area can be shown to be the work done which is $m\Delta V_{g}$:
$$
\begin{gather*}
\text{Area} \approx -\frac{GMm}{r^{2}}r  \\ \\
\approx -\frac{GM}{r}m \\ \\ 
\approx m\Delta V_{g}
\end{gather*}
$$
