The answer was B. My mistake was A. This was because although I factored in that $v=\frac{I}{Ane}$ and $v\propto   \frac{1}{A}$ I missed out that $I$ is directly affecting by the length of the wire. 

If wires are connected in parallel across a cell with negligible internal resistance, this means that the they have the same voltage across them.

$$
v = \frac{I}{Ane} \qquad \therefore v\propto \frac{I}{A}
$$

$$
\begin{gather*}
I = \frac{V}{R} \qquad R = \frac{\rho L}{A} \\ \\
I = \frac{AV}{pL} \implies I \propto \frac{A}{L}
\end{gather*}

$$
Where $\rho$ is resisitvity. Substituting back into equation for $v$:
$$
v\propto \frac{\frac{A}{L}}{A} \propto \frac{A}{AL} \propto \frac{1}{L}
$$
Recall that $\frac{\frac{x}{y}}{z} = \frac{x}{y} \times \frac{1}{z}$ and $\frac{\frac{a}{b}}{\frac{c}{d}} = \frac{a}{b} \times \frac{d}{c}$
