# Fluorine-18 
This versatile medical tracer is used in positron emission tomography. The isotope emits positrons and has half-life of 110 minutes. A nucleus of fluorine-18 decays into oxygen-18 in $\beta^{+}$ decay, emitting a gamma photon.
$$
^{18}_{9}F \to ^{18}_{8}O + ^{0}_{+1}e + v_{e} + \gamma 
$$
Fluorine is made by colliding high speed protons with oxygen-18. This collision produces fluorine-18 and free neutrons:
$$
^{1}_{1}p + ^{18}_{8}O \to ^{18}_{9}F + ^{1}_{0}n
$$
