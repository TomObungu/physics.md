The higher the frequency of black body radiation emitted will yield each photon having higher energy levels:
$$
\Delta E = hf
$$
We know that. This is the inverse square law
$$
I = \frac{P}{4\pi r^{2}}
$$
For a star of radius r: 
The Luminosity is the radiant power of electromagnetic radiation of the star:
$$
L = 4\pi r^{2}\sigma T^{4} = P
$$
Therefore substituting $L$ as P for the equation for intensity:
$$
I = \frac{4\pi r^{2}\sigma T^{4}}{4\pi r^{2}}
$$
This gives us:
$$
I = \sigma T^{4}
$$
