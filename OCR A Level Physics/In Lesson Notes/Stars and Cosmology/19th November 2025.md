# Hubble's Law and Hubble's Constant
## Cosmological redshift
We are able to detect cosmological redshift by detecting a significant shift in the spectra of the wave source from galaxies. The greater proportion of redshift allows us to estimate it's recessional velocity using the Doppler equation. 

- Key evidence for expanding universe that followed by the Big Bang theory
- Space-time is expanding in all directions
- The spacetime in-between galaxies is stretching
- The redshift on these scales is caused by the stretching of the emitted light as it travels an expanding universe.
