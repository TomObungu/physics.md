## Strong force
Inside protostars, when the molecules are moving fast enough, the atomic nuclei  which have protons with positive charge have a repulsive force called the strong force. The strong force only takes place when the atomic nuclei are very close to touching.  The molecules will need to be moving at a higher enough velocity to overcome the strong repulsive force.
