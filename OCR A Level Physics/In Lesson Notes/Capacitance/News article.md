It has been reported that capacitors are vital in computer circuits for filtering out noise, stabilising voltage and managing signals. Capacitors do this by working as tiny temporary batteries to provide quick power boosts or absorb voltage spikes. (Duff Wilmer, 1916)  Capacitors play an essential role in dynamic memory management in your every day devices. Capacitors do this by storing data as electrical charges, with a charged capacitor representing '1' and a discharged capacitor representing '0'. This method of representing dynamic memory is still used today (Floyd Thomas, 2005).
