Derivation for capacitance in series:
$$
\begin{gather*} 
V_{T} = V_{1} + V_{2} + V_{3} \dots \text{(Kirchoff's second law)} \\ \\ 
V = \frac{Q}{C} \\ \\
\therefore V= \frac{Q}{C_{T}}  = \frac{Q}{C_{1}} +\frac{Q}{C_{2}} + \frac{Q}{C_{3}}\dots \\ \\
\therefore \frac{1}{C_{T}} = \frac{1}{C_{1}} + \frac{1}{C_{2}} + \frac{1}{C_{3}}\dots
\end{gather*}
$$
Derivation for capacitance in parallel:
$$
\begin{gather*}
I_{T} = I_{1} + I_{2} + I_{3} \dots (\text{Kirchoff's first law}) \\ \\ 
Q \propto I \\ \\
\therefore Q_{T} = Q_{1} + Q_{2} + Q_{3} \dots \\ \\
Q_{T} = VC_{T} \\ \\ (\text{Voltage is the same in a parallel circuit}) \\ \\
\therefore  VC_{T} =  VC_{1} + VC_{2} +VC_{3}\dots \\ \\
\therefore C_{T} = C_{1} + C_{2} + C_{3}\dots
\end{gather*} 
$$
