Full Einstien special relativity equation equation
$$
E^{2} = \rho^{2}c^{2} + M_{0}^{2}c^{4}
$$
For massless particles like photons:
$$
\frac{E^{2}}{c^{2}} = p^{2} 
$$
Thus:
$$
p = \frac{E}{c}
$$
Therefore:
$$
E = \frac{hf}{c}
$$
