# What causes bar magnets to have magnetic fields
![[Pasted image 20260107115417.png]]
Within the domains of a bar magnet, there are regions that have polarity. Inside these regions, the atoms have electron spin. The electrons have an intrinsic angular spin. When there is a balance of magnetic spin, there is a net magnetic fields. 

This means magnetism could be pseudo-force as it relies on the basis of electrically charged electrons
