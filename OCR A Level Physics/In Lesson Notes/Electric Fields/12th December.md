# Electric Potential and Electric Potential Energy 
## Electric Potential Derivation
Electric potential is defined as the work required to move a positive test charge from infinity to a point r away from the centre of a charge.  ![[Pasted image 20251212095137.png]]
From the definition of voltage:
$$
\begin{gather*}
V = \frac{W}{Q} = \frac{E}{q}
\end{gather*}
$$
Thus:
$$
V = \frac{Qq}{4\pi\varepsilon_{0}r}\left( \frac{1}{q} \right) = \frac{Q}{4\pi\varepsilon_{0}r}
$$
Thus we can see that electric Potential $V$ is inversely proportional to the distance between the plates $r$:
## Equipotential
In a unform field, charges along a horizontal line with a distance of $r$ above the negative terminal will have the same electric potential. 

However, in a radial field, the lines in where the distance is the same is a circular radius around the charge.
