## Starter Workbook Corrections:
Electric field - Region of space where a charge will experience an electrostatic force
Electric field strength - The force generated per unit charge on a positive test mass placed on that point
Dipole - A pair of opposite sign charges separated by a small distance, e.g. like a molar molecule like (HCl)
Direction Of Electric field - Always shows the direction a positive test charge will flow in.
