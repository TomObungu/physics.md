## Equipotential radial fields
You can see that there is a radial field around the point mass where the $Vg$ is the same.
![[Pasted image 20251022123832.png]]

 This means that if you move around a line of equipotential energy then the $Vg$ is the same:
  This applies for an object that does not have circular equipotential lines:
  ![[Pasted image 20251022124718.png]]
  This means the $Vg$ is the same as the point on $Vg_{1}$ and $Vg_{2}$ regardless of the radius because it is on a line of equipotential energy. The elipitcal shape of the field lines could be due to the point mass body having multiple bodies distorting the uniform field or the point mass body having a a non-uniform body. If you were to draw the lines you would see that the strength of the gravitational field is stronger at some points. Therefore changing the radius will keep the $Vg$ constant
