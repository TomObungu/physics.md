| Part                | Property                                                                                        | Function                                                                         |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fuel rods           | Rods containing enriched fissile fuel.                                                          | Provide the fissile fuel for thermionic neutrons to start a chain reaction with  |
| Control rods        | ~~Need to be cheap and readily available.~~ Made of a material that absorbs neutrons            | Absorb the excess neutrons from fission                                          |
| Moderator           | Must not absorb neutrons - only slow them down. Need to be cheap and readily available.         | Slow down the product neutrons of fission to be suitable thermionic neutrons     |
| Coolant             | Often used as the moderator. Large specific heat capacity to effectively cool.                  | Cool the reactor core to prevent excess heat being formed from fissile reactions |
| Radiation Sheidling | Must be thick and dense to prevent all forms of radiation from reaching the outside environemtn | Prevent contamination of radiation into the outside  environment                 |
