8.3 Kirchoff's first law
The sum of current into a point is the sum of a current out of that point. The law is based on conservation of charge - charge cannot be destroyed so the same amount of charge carries entering that point must be equal to the same number of charge carries leaving that point

9.7 Resistivity
Electrical property of a material. It measures how strongly a material opposes electric current. Unit is $\Omega m$Increasing length will increase the p.d across it. Therefore the resistance must be doubled. 
$$
R = \frac{\rho L}{A}
$$
Therefore resistivity is:
$$
 \rho = \frac{RA}{L}
$$
We know that R is proportional to the length $L$ of a wire and inversely proportional to the area of a wire $A$:
$$
R \propto \frac{L}{A}
$$
Resistivity is the constant of proportionality for that equation.
The resistivity can be worked out using the circuit diagram and by varying the length of the wire and measuring the resistance. A graph of R against L will the $\rho$
